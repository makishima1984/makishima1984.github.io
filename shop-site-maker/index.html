Publish

  1. Download index.html and put it alone in a new folder (one folder per client).
  2. Open app.netlify.com/drop and drag that folder onto the page. You get a free link like something.netlify.app.
  3. Open the link on your own phone and check every button. Then send it to your client.

If the link stops working, sign in to Netlify and re-publish the site.
