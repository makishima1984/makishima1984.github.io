How many calories in jalebi?

~150 kcal  ·  1 g protein
for 1 piece

What that means

  • About the same as 1.4 rotis (one medium roti is ~110 kcal).
  • Roughly 38 minutes of brisk walking to use it up (very rough, depends on your weight).
  • That is about 8% of a 2,000 kcal day.
