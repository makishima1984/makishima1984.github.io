How many calories in banana?

~105 kcal  ·  1 g protein
for 1 banana

What that means

  • About the same as 1.0 roti (one medium roti is ~110 kcal).
  • Roughly 26 minutes of brisk walking to use it up (very rough, depends on your weight).
  • That is about 5% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Close to it

  • Roti / chapati (1 medium): ~110 kcal
  • Chai with milk and sugar (1 cup): ~100 kcal
  • Roasted chana (30 g): ~110 kcal
  • Apple (1): ~95 kcal

Lighter swaps

  • Moong sprouts (1 cup): ~30 kcal
  • Idli (1): ~50 kcal
  • Orange (1): ~62 kcal
