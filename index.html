How these work

Your data never leaves your device

There is no account and no server. Each tool saves what you type inside your own browser, and the trackers have buttons to download a backup or delete everything.

To keep a tool on your phone, open it, then use your browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen".

Clearing your browser data deletes what you saved, so download a backup now and then.
