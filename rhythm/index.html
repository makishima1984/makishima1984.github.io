IDEA 18 · PROTOTYPE STAGE

Learn by copying.
Keep trying.

The author’s idea is broad: learn anything by copying and trying, including singing, instruments, sports and anything else.

What this version explores

A proposed beginner rhythm scenario: listen to an original generated pattern, copy it with taps, inspect browser timing, and retry. Three small exercises, adjustable pace, a short recall mode and a local journal are functional.

What the feedback means

Timing is measured relative to your first tap. It ignores starting delay and pairs taps in order. The comparison is a rough practice aid; input and audio latency are not calibrated. A good match here does not prove retention, transfer or mastery.

What is sample or unavailable

All exercises are original sample content. “Explore a sample result” uses labelled synthetic timings and is excluded from your journal. There is no microphone, camera, AI coach, teacher review, cloud account, external lesson library, or singing/sports assessment.

Research and scope

This slice follows the supplied context and the research handoff dated 11 October 2026. The demonstration → attempt → feedback loop and choice of rhythm practice are design proposals, not an author-confirmed app specification. A future working application needs device timing validation and a study of learning outcomes.

Your entries stay in this browser when storage is available. Clear the journal at any time. No external services, fonts or tracking are used.
